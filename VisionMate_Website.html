VisionMate is a proposed wearable assistant concept that turns selected visual information into clear audio descriptions and navigation support for people who are blind or have low vision.

Discover how it worksView features

A student entrepreneurship concept focused on independence, accessibility and responsible innovation.

THE CHALLENGE

Everyday places can be hard to interpret without sight.

Finding a sign, recognising an object or understanding an unfamiliar environment can require extra time and support. Navigation apps help with routes, but do not necessarily describe nearby objects.

  • ✓Understanding objects and signs in unfamiliar places
  • ✓Getting useful information without looking at a screen
  • ✓Accessing spoken route information while moving

RESPONSIBLE INNOVATION

Safety comes first

VisionMate is a concept under development, not a certified mobility device.

Important: AI can miss objects or misunderstand a scene. The prototype must not be relied on to decide whether a path is safe or to replace a white cane, guide dog or established mobility skills. Any trials should be supervised and co-designed with visually impaired users and accessibility professionals.
OUR MISSION

More information. More confidence. More independence.

We are exploring how responsible AI can make everyday information more accessible.
